# v43 Archive 2022 — Round 1 Filter

- Archive discovery now accepts only matches whose Flashscore `data-start-time` is within the 2022 final tournament window: 2022-11-20 through 2022-12-18.
- Qualification and pre-tournament matches before Round 1 are excluded from the generated queue.
- Expected queue size remains 64 matches, ordered chronologically from the opening match through the final.
- Non-archive discovery behavior is unchanged.
